## The hidden cost of manual meeting notes

Manual typing splits your attention during moments that require full presence: reading body language during a hiring decision, catching hesitation in a customer's feature request, noticing when a board member pauses on a strategic question. The in-meeting distraction costs you twice: first in reduced engagement quality, then in the time required to reconstruct context you missed while typing.

Post-meeting cleanup adds another layer. If even 10% of your meeting time goes to documentation overhead, you lose half a day weekly.

## Beyond the math: Institutional memory and confidentiality

Time savings represent measurable ROI, but two qualitative benefits matter just as much: you preserve knowledge when employees leave, and you can document confidential conversations without visible recording technology.

When your Head of Engineering gives notice, their context usually walks out with them. Customer pain points they heard in sales engineering calls, architectural decisions made in technical reviews, feature requests mentioned casually in Slack huddles. All of that institutional memory vanishes unless it was explicitly documented. Granola creates a searchable archive so departures create knowledge gaps rather than complete knowledge loss.

## Frequently asked questions about Granola ROI

### How accurate is the 20-minute time savings estimate?

The calculator uses 20 minutes as a baseline for meeting overhead (including prep, in-meeting distraction, and post-meeting cleanup). Based on internal estimates, actual savings typically range from 15-40 minutes depending on meeting complexity and workflow.

### Does Granola work for in-person meetings?

Yes, the iOS mobile app captures audio from your phone's microphone during in-person conversations.

### Is my data secure and private?

We're SOC 2 Type 2 certified and GDPR compliant, audio is transcribed then deleted, and AI providers can't train on your data. All users can opt out of model training in settings.

### Can I replay the audio from past meetings?

No, we [don't store audio recordings](https://www.granola.ai/security), only text transcripts. If your compliance team requires audio verification, Granola won't meet that need.

### How long does setup take?

You'll be up and running in under 5 minutes: [download the app](https://notes.granola.ai/download), sign in with Google, grant microphone permissions, and connect your calendar.

## Key terminology

**Institutional memory:** The collective knowledge of your organization that persists beyond individual employee tenure, captured in searchable meeting records.

**Bot-free capture:** Capturing device audio locally without a virtual participant joining the video call, eliminating the visible "recording started" announcement.

**Enhanced notes:** The combination of your rough notes written during the meeting and AI-generated context drawn from the full transcript.
