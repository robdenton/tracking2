Accounting professionals are adopting AI across a wide range of tasks: Tax research, advisory work, and predictive planning. But regardless of where firms start with AI, the bigger time drain frequently lies elsewhere. Meeting-related admin work, from post-meeting write-ups to filing notes and logging action items, quietly absorbs hours that could go toward billable advisory work. The question is not whether that time adds up. It is whether your documentation process is built to recover it.

An [AI meeting assistant](https://www.granola.ai/ai-meeting-assistant) for accountants is software that captures spoken conversation during client calls, transcribes the audio using speech recognition and natural language processing, and converts the transcript into structured notes, action items, and summaries. Some tools generate those outputs fully automatically. Others take a human-in-the-loop approach where you jot rough notes during the call and the AI enhances them with transcript context afterward.

For accounting professionals, either approach means translating technical tax discussions into plain language client summaries, capturing commitments made across multiple engagements, and building a searchable record of advisory conversations that survives staff turnover. The question is not whether to adopt one. It is which architecture fits how your firm actually works.

## Why accountants are adopting AI meeting assistants

The accounting profession is shifting. The revenue opportunity and the client value now sit firmly in advisory services: Tax planning, financial strategy, M&A guidance, and business performance conversations. That shift creates a documentation problem that most firms have not solved.

### The shift to advisory services

Capturing notes and action items from meetings is a documented productivity drain across professional services firms, and the issue grows when responsibilities and deadlines go unrecorded. For advisory-led firms, that fragmentation scales across dozens of active engagements, where a single missed commitment or undocumented concern can cost the relationship.

### Translating technical discussions into client language

Most clients do not understand tax code. They understand impact. AI meeting assistants capture the technical discussion and generate a plain English summary of the implications, closing the gap between what you know and what your client retains. When clients leave a call unclear on what was discussed or what happens next, trust erodes, and advisory relationships are built on trust. Accurate post-meeting documentation is a retention tool as much as a productivity one.

### Action item tracking across engagements

A common pattern in advisory practices is that notes live in one place, tasks in another, and client emails somewhere else entirely, making it difficult to reconstruct what was agreed and when. At five or ten active advisory clients, that fragmentation is manageable. At 30 or 50 clients, it is a structural problem. An AI meeting assistant that captures commitments, extracts action items, and makes them searchable across all engagements prevents the repeated conversations and missed follow-throughs that erode client trust.

## Two categories of AI meeting tools for accountants

Two distinct categories define the market. Understanding the architectural difference between them determines which one fits your firm's needs.

### Horizontal AI notepads: Granola, Otter, Fathom

Horizontal tools are built to work across any meeting type, any platform, and any industry.

- **Granola** is an AI notepad for back-to-back meetings. You jot rough notes during the call and Granola enhances them with transcript context afterward. It captures device audio directly, so no participant appears in your video call. Business plan pricing is $14/user/month.
- **Otter.ai** auto-joins calls as a visible participant through its OtterPilot feature and holds SOC 2 Type 2 certification. Pricing starts at $8.33/month annually for Pro and $19.99/month annually for Business.
- **Fathom** and **Fireflies** offer additional horizontal options with bot-based capture as the default. Fireflies joins calls as a visible bot with "Notetaker" appearing in the participant list. Fathom's default behavior is also visible bot capture, though the product now includes a bot-free option.

### Vertical practice tools: Vinyl, Canopy, Ping

Vertical tools are built specifically for accounting workflows, with terminology and practice management integrations designed for CPAs from the ground up.

- **Canopy Notetaker** is integrated within Canopy's practice management platform, providing encrypted storage, role-based access, and audit logging. It generates accounting-aware summaries that highlight client intent, advisory needs, potential risks, specific commitments, and follow-up actions, and automatically files them into the correct client record.
- **Ping** serves advisory-focused accounting firms by automating post-meeting admin work, including transcription and task entry processes tied to client workflows.
- **Vinyl** focuses on accounting-specific meeting documentation and integrates directly with practice management workflows, including automatic filing and action item creation tied to client files.

### Key architectural differences

Three architectural differences separate horizontal and vertical tools: How they integrate with practice management software, how they generate summaries, and how they appear to meeting participants. On that last point, bot-based tools join your video call as a visible participant. Everyone in the meeting sees "Notetaker" in the participant list. When that happens, conversation dynamics shift: Sensitive feedback, candid frustrations, and off-the-record observations are less likely to surface. For advisory conversations where client trust depends on discretion, this visibility matters.

Granola works differently. It uses your device's audio, so there's no bot to interrupt the call. You decide which meetings to capture, and Granola makes it easy to let clients know when you do, with features like a visible watermark or an automatic chat message. The audio is transcribed in real time and then deleted immediately. Only the transcript and your enhanced notes persist.

## When horizontal AI assistants win

### Firm intelligence across all client conversations

Firm intelligence is the cumulative knowledge your practice builds from every client interaction: The tax strategies you have recommended, the concerns clients have raised, the commitments you have made, and the patterns that emerge across engagements over time. Centralizing that knowledge lets team members access the firm's collective expertise instantly, improving recommendation quality and reducing duplicated research. When that intelligence is locked inside individual notepads or scattered across systems, it disappears when a staff member leaves.

Horizontal tools like Granola enable this through folder-level queries. You create shared folders for client segments, service lines, or partner conversations. Then you query across all of them: "What tax concerns did our manufacturing clients raise this quarter?" The answers come back with source-linked citations from specific meetings.

## FAQs

**Can clients tell I'm using an AI meeting assistant?**

With bot-free tools like Granola, no bot appears in the call, but Granola offers built-in ways to let clients know when notes are being transcribed, such as a
visible watermark or an automatic chat message. 

**Do I need client consent to transcribe calls?**

Consent requirements vary by jurisdiction and are the firm's responsibility to determine, so check with your team admin or legal team on what types of calls
you can transcribe. On the product side, Granola uses your device's audio, so there's no bot to interrupt the call, and Granola offers built-in ways to let clients know when transcription is happening, such as a visible watermark or an automatic chat message. Granola's participant privacy guidance covers what the product does and does not do. Confirm specific requirements with your own counsel.

**How does Granola integrate with my practice management software?**

Granola does not integrate natively with accounting practice management platforms like Canopy or TaxDome. **What happens to my meeting notes if I cancel my Granola plan?**

**Is Granola SOC 2 Type 2 certified?**

## Key terms glossary

**AI meeting assistant:** Software that captures spoken conversation during calls, transcribes audio using speech recognition and natural language processing, and converts it into structured notes and action items.

**Firm intelligence:** The cumulative knowledge an accounting practice builds from every client interaction, including recommended strategies, client concerns, commitments made, and patterns that emerge across engagements over time. When structured and searchable, it becomes a firm-wide asset rather than an individual one.

**Practice management software:** Integrated platforms designed for accounting firms to manage client relationships, workflows, billing, document storage, and compliance tasks in one system.

**SOC 2 Type 2:** A security attestation that verifies an organization's controls for data privacy, confidentiality, and security have been independently audited over a sustained period rather than at a single point in time.
