## Glossary

**Device-level audio capture:** A transcription architecture that captures and transcribes device audio from the host computer's microphone and system speakers without joining the meeting as a visible participant. Granola offers built-in ways to let other participants know when transcription is in use.

**Human-in-the-loop**: A note-taking paradigm where the user provides structure or rough notes during the meeting and AI fills in supporting detail from the transcript afterward, rather than generating a fully automated summary with no user input.

**Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: An open standard that allows AI applications to connect to external data sources and tools. Granola's MCP support enables meeting notes to be queried by compatible AI tools.

**SOC 2 Type 2**: A security certification issued by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA) that verifies a company's controls around security, availability, and data confidentiality have been audited and validated over a sustained observation period.
