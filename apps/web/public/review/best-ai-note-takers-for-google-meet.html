Evaluating AI note takers for Google Meet

The right AI note taker for Google Meet depends on four practical questions:

  • How accurate and useful are the notes it produces?
  • How easily can you retrieve information from past conversations?
  • How well does it push structured data into your CRM or workflow tools?
  • Does it affect participant behavior in the meetings where candor matters?5

Each of these factors varies significantly across the tools available today.

Professionals in meeting-heavy roles move between calls continuously: Sales discovery, customer research, recruiting interviews, internal standups, executive briefings. At that volume, the quality of your notes compounds quickly. A tool that produces generic summaries, stores notes in a silo with no CRM path, or subtly changes how participants show up in sensitive conversations creates a documentation gap that grows wider every week6.

Standardized capture for hybrid workflows

Conversations don't stay inside one meeting platform. A team standup runs on Zoom. A client call happens on Google Meet. A recruiting debrief is in person. A stakeholder update is on a direct phone line. Platform-specific tools break the documentation chain the moment a conversation moves outside their supported environment, forcing you to switch tools mid-workflow.

Integrating with your current CRM

HubSpot, Attio, Affinity, and Notion sit at the center of most relationship and pipeline workflows. When meeting notes live in a separate tool with no pathway to the CRM, contact data goes stale. Context from the conversation doesn't reach the record. The pattern recognition that should inform a follow-up or decision gets buried in a search nobody runs.

Prioritizing privacy13 in AI note taking

One factor that affects note quality indirectly is how the tool is perceived by the other people in the meeting. A customer research participant who notices a bot in the participant list may give polished, guarded answers rather than honest feedback.14 A job candidate may hesitate to discuss concerns about a role. A partner or client may hold back on sensitive context.15

The visible participant changes the dynamic before a single question is asked.16 Where those dynamics matter, professionals running confidential interviews, diligence calls or executive discussions often treat bot-free capture as a baseline requirement rather than a premium feature17,18.

Native Gemini integration for Google Meet notes

Google offers a native AI feature inside Google Meet that is among the most integrated options available for Google Workspace users. It generates notes without a visible bot participant and saves them automatically to Google Drive, which makes it worth understanding in detail before evaluating alternatives.19,20

How Gemini captures meeting context

Gemini's "Take notes for me" feature operates inside the Google Meet infrastructure, so it doesn't require a separate app or any visible participant21. Gemini generates notes after the meeting ends and saves them as a Google Doc in the meeting organizer's Drive folder.

For a detailed technical overview of the feature, Google's native Meet help documentation covers the exact eligibility requirements and output format.

Gemini's strengths for Google Meet

Deep ecosystem integration is the real advantage here. Notes appear in Drive, link back to the Calendar event, and share automatically22 with attendees who are inside your Google Workspace organization. For teams already running on Google Workspace who primarily meet internally, the friction of adding an external tool is nonexistent.

Limitations of native Gemini notes

The eligibility requirements are narrower than most teams expect, with the feature reportedly available on higher-tier Google Workspace plans. The feature supports multiple languages, with one language per meeting.

The deeper limitation is the absence of customization. Generic summaries typically lack template structure, CRM integration, and the ability to query across multiple meetings. You can't ask "What did every enterprise customer say about their onboarding experience this quarter?" and get a sourced answer. You get a document per meeting, not a knowledge base across meetings.

Optimizing Google Workspace meeting notes

If Gemini is your only option due to organizational constraints, the most effective approach is to treat its output as a first draft. After each meeting, open the generated Google Doc and structure it manually using a consistent framework: Context, key decisions, risks, and follow-up commitments. Build a Notion database row from each structured doc to create searchable meeting history.

FAQs

Do AI note takers require a visible bot to join Google Meet?

No. Granola, an AI notepad rather than a traditional note taker, captures device audio locally through your computer's system audio, so no visible participant joins the call56,57. You can enable an automated chat message or a video watermark so participants know Granola is active.58,59 Other bot-free60 tools operate on a similar principle. Native tools like Gemini also avoid a visible bot but are limited to Google Meet and eligible Workspace plans.61

Is Granola SOC 2 Type 2 compliant62?

Yes. Granola achieved SOC 2 Type 2 certification in July 2025, and its architecture deletes audio immediately after transcription, with third-party AI providers contractually prohibited from training on customer data.

Can I use Gemini notes on a basic Google Workspace plan?

No. Native Gemini meeting notes require a Business or Enterprise Workspace plan, or an eligible Google AI plan. Basic Google Workspace plans and free accounts are not eligible. Check Google's current plan eligibility page for the most up-to-date tier list.

Which CRMs does Granola integrate with?

Granola's Business plan includes direct integrations with Affinity, HubSpot, and Attio, plus Notion, Slack, and Zapier for connecting to additional tools in your workflow.

Can Granola capture audio from Google Meet and Zoom in the same workflow?

Yes. Granola captures system audio from your device, which means it works identically across Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, and any other platform where audio plays through your computer. You don't need separate tools for different meeting platforms.

What happens to meeting audio after Granola transcribes it?

Granola deletes audio after transcription. Only the text transcript and your enhanced notes persist. Granola stores no audio files in its infrastructure, which is a core part of its privacy63 architecture and what allowed the firm to complete SOC 2 Type 2 certification in three months rather than the typical 12-18 months.

Key terminology

Bot-free64 capture: A transcription method that captures system or device audio locally65 from your computer, eliminating the need for a virtual participant to join the video call66.

Bot-based capture: A transcription method where a virtual participant joins the video call, appears in the participant list, and captures audio through the meeting platform67.

Human-in-the-loop: A product design approach where your manual inputs during the meeting (rough notes, keywords, or bullets) anchor and guide the AI's enhancement, ensuring the output reflects your priorities rather than a generic summary of everything said.

Agentic chat: An AI chat interface capable of querying across multiple meetings simultaneously, identifying patterns, and providing source-linked citations from specific conversations. Granola's agentic chat handles both quick factual questions ("What were the action items from Tuesday's call?") and complex analytical queries ("What themes appear across every enterprise SaaS pitch from Q1?").

System audio capture: The process of transcribing audio directly from a computer's operating system rather than through a meeting platform integration. System audio capture enables compatibility with any meeting tool, including those without native API access.
